Privacy Policy for HabitZero

Effective Date: November 11, 2026 • Version 1.0
The HabitZero Privacy Guarantee: HabitZero is built from the ground up as a zero-cloud, 100% offline habit tracking application. We do not operate remote tracking servers, do not require user accounts, do not collect personal identifiers, and do not include third-party advertising SDKs or analytics trackers. All data stays strictly on your physical device.

1. Information We Collect (Zero Personal Data)

HabitZero does not collect, transmit, sell, or rent any personal information to external servers or third parties. Specifically:

  • No Account Required: You never register, log in, or provide your name, phone number, or email to use the application.
  • No Location Tracking: HabitZero does not access GPS or coarse network location data.
  • No Cloud Storage: All habit records, abstinence stopwatches, urge logs, triggers, journal entries, and financial savings calculations are stored exclusively in a local SQLite/Room database inside your device’s private app sandbox.

2. On-Device Data Storage & Security

All data generated by your use of HabitZero resides on your local device storage:

  • Application Sandbox: Data is protected by standard Android operating system sandbox permissions, preventing other apps from accessing your records.
  • Data Backup & Export: If you use the manual JSON backup feature, the backup file is exported strictly to a folder of your choosing via the native Android Storage Access Framework (SAF). HabitZero never uploads backups to any cloud.
  • Complete Data Deletion: Uninstalling HabitZero immediately and permanently deletes all habit data, statistics, and logs from your device. You can also wipe data at any time via Android Settings > Apps > HabitZero > Clear Storage.

3. Third-Party Libraries & Services

HabitZero minimizes third-party dependencies to preserve complete privacy:

  • Google Play In-App Review API: We utilize Google's standard Play Review library to allow users to optionally rate the app on the Google Play Store. This interaction is handled entirely by Google Play Services according to Google's standard privacy policies. No habit data is sent with the review prompt.
  • No Advertising SDKs: HabitZero contains zero ad networks (no AdMob, no Unity Ads, no Facebook Audience Network).
  • No Analytics SDKs: HabitZero contains zero telemetry or user analytics trackers (no Firebase Analytics, no Mixpanel, no Adjust).

4. Device Permissions Used

HabitZero requests only the minimum Android permissions strictly required for offline functionality:

  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Used exclusively to deliver optional daily check-in reminders scheduled locally by the user. Notifications are created locally via Android WorkManager/AlarmManager with no remote push servers.
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED: Used solely to reschedule your local daily reminders if your phone is restarted.

5. Children’s Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

HabitZero does not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. Since the app collects no personal information whatsoever, it complies fully with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play's Families Policy.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any revisions will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
